# V007: a voice missing, Mitra's own, or saying twice

Every measurement and review says who saw or said it, its voice:
`human:ana`, `agent:…`, `machine:tool`, or `test:name` for a test run.
Independent voices are what weigh, so one voice reviews one proposition
once. `model` and `machine:mitra-check` are Mitra's own voices (what
the model alone says, what Mitra checked): explain takes with them, the
ledger does not.

## Example

    review stone::air holds by "human:ana";
    review stone::air fails by "human:ana";    // error[V007]: `human:ana` reviews `stone::air` twice
    review stone::air holds by "model";        // error[V007]: `model` is Mitra's own voice

## Fix

Name the voice, and keep one review per voice and proposition.
