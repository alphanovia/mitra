# B002: more diagnoses than blame looked for

Blame gives the smallest diagnoses first, up to `Settings::diagnoses`
(default 10), trying at most `Settings::tries` sets of suspects (default
100,000) in each group of conflicts that share suspects. It found one
more than it gives, or stopped at the budget: more diagnoses exist, or
may, as small or larger.

## Example

    // blame with Settings { diagnoses: 1, .. }, two failed tests
    // finding[B002]: the first 1 diagnoses only: more may exist
    // (`Settings::diagnoses`, `Settings::tries`)

## Fix

Raise the settings, or settle the first diagnoses (fix or review what
they drop) and blame again.
