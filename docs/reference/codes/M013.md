# M013: a declaration that rests on itself

A conclusion rests on its rule's premises and on the rule, a require on
its ensure, and an ensure on what its lists name. On a cycle, no
proposition would be a leaf: only a taking of something on it would hold
any of it. Each declaration on the cycle is reported, across parts too.

## Example

In the illustrative syntax of `docs/design/model.md`:

    rule again: drop::slow |- drop::slow;    // error[M013]

## Fix

Break the cycle: let one of them rest on something else, or on a
taking.
