# M009: a require from no ensure of another part

A require comes from words, a hypothesis, or from what another part
ensures. Not from a claim or a function, not from nothing, and not from an
ensure of its own part, which a rule can name as it is.

## Example

In the illustrative syntax of `docs/model.md`:

    require light = sun::light;    // error[M009]: a function, not an ensure

## Fix

Name an ensure of another part, or say it in words.
