commands::conform::correct | holds | It ties through reader.tie with the rev, writes the found evidence to the ledger via Store::replace("reader:"), counts the untied pieces of subjects (dropping boundary pieces), and returns Ok(1) exactly when ties differ or a piece is untied, Ok(0) otherwise, with reader/store failures as Err.
commands::conform::deterministic | holds | Its result and output come only from the store's files, the ledger and what reader.tie/reader.tests return, with Vec iteration and an order-independent line count, so the same inputs and state give the same output.
commands::check_rev::correct | holds | It returns Err for the empty string, anything starting with `-` (so "--source"), and anything holding `"`, `\n` or `\r`, and Ok otherwise (so "abc123"), which matches every claim and the refusal list in rev_is_safe_to_write (taking "quotes" as the ledger's double quote).
commands::check_rev::deterministic | holds | It is a pure function of its string argument with no state.
commands::commands_only::captured | fails | No claims or requires are listed for this ensure, and no claim says anything about which crates the command line reads, writes or judges through.

commands::build_says_nothing_owed::captured | fails | No claims are listed for it, and nothing checks what `build` reports, that it writes the build files, or that it says nothing of the evidence owed.

commands::exit_says_it::captured | fails | No claims are listed for it, and no claim relates exit codes 0/1/2 to the outcome of a command.
